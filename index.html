Frequently Asked Questions

Is Voxium free?

Yes. Voxium is free to download and use. The included on-device voice is free after its one-time model download. Optional cloud voices use your own account and API key with the provider you choose, so any provider charges are between you and that provider. An optional one-off purchase, Voxium Pro, unlocks CarPlay, Keep Playing, speed and highlight colour controls on the reading screen, the Apple Watch app, Scan a Page, export presets, and alternate app icons — everything else stays free.

What is Voxium Pro?

Voxium Pro is a single one-off in-app purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks CarPlay, Keep Playing, speed and highlight colour controls on the reading screen, the Apple Watch app, Scan a Page, export presets, and alternate app icons. Buy it on your iPhone or iPad, and your Apple Watch picks it up from the phone. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID — use "Restore Purchases" on the Voxium Pro screen to bring it to a new device. Payment, receipts, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

The Apple Watch app is part of Voxium Pro and needs watchOS 11 or later. It is installed with the iPhone app. Free users do not see a Watch app.

As a remote, it controls playback on your iPhone: play and pause, back 15 seconds and forward 30 seconds, previous and next chapter, your library, a chapter list, and the sleep timer. The Watch shows the title and how far you are through the book. Sound plays through the iPhone or its headphones.

If you have exported a book as audio on the iPhone, you can copy it to the Watch and play it there, through the Watch speaker or AirPods, without taking the iPhone with you. Your place stays in sync on both devices. If they are out of range, the Watch passes it on the next time they connect. Cloud voices cannot play live on the Watch; only a copied export plays from the Watch itself.

How does CarPlay work?

CarPlay is part of Voxium Pro. You can listen to your books in the car, with back 15 and forward 30 buttons on the Now Playing screen. Keep Playing also works in the car. CarPlay opens on the book that is already playing; if nothing is playing, it opens on your library. Chapter skipping is not available in CarPlay.

What is Keep Playing?

Keep Playing is part of Voxium Pro. When a document plays to the end, the next one in your library starts automatically. It follows the library as shown (newest import first), skips documents you have already finished, and stops at the bottom of the list. It works with the phone locked, with AirPods, and in CarPlay, and the sleep timer carries on from one document to the next. To turn it on or off, use the "Keep playing my library" switch on the Voxium Pro screen.

How do I scan a printed page?

Scan a Page is part of Voxium Pro. In Add Document, choose Scan Page to read printed pages with the camera. Voxium uses the system document camera, so it can straighten and crop the page and capture several pages in one go. The text is read on your device and the scan is never uploaded. It then goes straight into your library as a document, ready to play. The first time you use it, Voxium asks for permission to use the camera.

How do I switch voices on the reading screen?

A voice pill above the player shows the current voice. The arrows on either side step through your favourite voices for the current provider, or you can tap the name to pick from your favourites list. This is free for everyone.

How do I skip through a book, and how do I skip a chapter?

The skip buttons on the player are back 15 seconds and forward 30 seconds. A skip always lands at the start of a sentence. The same two buttons appear in the app, on the lock screen and on the Home Screen widget, and, with Voxium Pro, in CarPlay and on the Apple Watch.

To skip a chapter on iPhone or iPad, press and hold back 15 or forward 30. Voxium finds chapters from the headings in the text and shows the name of the chapter you have landed on. This is free. On Apple Watch (Voxium Pro), you can use the previous and next chapter controls, or pick a chapter from the chapter list. There is no separate chapter search field, and holding the skip buttons in CarPlay does not skip a chapter.

How do I change the reading speed?

A speed button on the reading screen is part of Voxium Pro. One tap changes the reading speed without opening Settings. It offers only the speeds your chosen voice provider supports. Free users can see the button; tapping it opens the Voxium Pro screen.

Can I change the highlight colour?

A palette button on the reading screen is part of Voxium Pro. It lets you choose the colour that highlights the sentence being read. Free users keep white; tapping the button opens the Voxium Pro screen.

Can I skip with my AirPods?

Yes. A double press skips forward 30 seconds, and a triple press goes back 15 seconds. This works when you are listening on the iPhone, and on the Apple Watch, whether you are controlling the phone or playing a book saved on the Watch through AirPods.

Do I need an account or API key?

No. You can use Voxium's on-device voice without an account or API key. The first time you use it, Voxium offers to download its voice model. You may also add your own MiniMax, ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, or Soniox API key if you prefer one of those cloud voices — Soniox includes their shared voice catalogue and your own cloned voices. Alternatively, sign in with your xAI account to use the xAI Grok voices on your existing xAI subscription. A Groq key is optional and is only used if you choose webpage cleanup.

What happens to my documents?

Imported documents and reading progress are stored on your device. If iCloud is available, Voxium can sync your library and settings through your private iCloud account so they are available on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Pages you scan with Scan a Page are stored in your library in the same way, as a document on your device.

Does Voxium send my text anywhere?

The on-device voice processes text on your device. If you select a cloud voice, Voxium sends the text needed to generate speech directly to that provider using your own API key. If you enable Groq webpage cleanup, webpage text is sent to Groq for that optional feature. When you use Scan a Page, the text is read on your device and the scan is never uploaded.

Can I use Voxium offline?

Yes. Once the on-device voice model has been downloaded, it can read imported or pasted text offline. With any voice, you can also export a book as audio and play that file offline, including from the Apple Watch after you have copied the export to the Watch. You need an internet connection to import webpages, sync with iCloud, download the voice model, or listen with a cloud voice in the app.

Can I export audio?

Yes. Voxium can export a document as an audio file stored on your device. When you use a cloud voice, export uses the same provider and may use that provider's credits. Only export material you have the right to use and share. With Voxium Pro, a book you have exported on the iPhone can be copied to the Apple Watch and played there.

What devices are supported?

Voxium is designed for iPhone and iPad, and needs iOS 18.0 or later. With Voxium Pro, you can also listen in the car through CarPlay, and there is an Apple Watch app for watchOS 11 or later. The Watch app is part of Voxium Pro, so it does not appear for free users.
